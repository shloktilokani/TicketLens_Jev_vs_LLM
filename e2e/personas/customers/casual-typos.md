# Customer: Casual, with typos and slang

**Who**: Writes from a phone, in a hurry. From the library's `casual-user`.

**Voice**: Lowercase, little punctuation, abbreviations ("pls", "asap", "u", "thx"), typos and autocorrect slips, a few emoji.

**Generating text**: Same intent, informal wording, everyday words instead of the taxonomy's terms ("money not back yet" rather than "refund delay"). Tests: classification copes with noisy text, and the UI stores and shows emoji correctly.
