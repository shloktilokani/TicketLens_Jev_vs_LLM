# Customer: Angry and impatient

**Who**: A customer whose problem has dragged on, who has probably contacted support before.

**Voice**: Short, heated sentences. CAPITALS for emphasis, repeated punctuation ("!!!"), threats to leave or post a review, sarcasm ("great service as always"). No insults or slurs.

**Generating text**: Keep the core problem clear but buried under frustration. Mention earlier contacts or how long it's been. Tests: does classification pick the underlying issue, not just "Complaint", and is sentiment Negative?
