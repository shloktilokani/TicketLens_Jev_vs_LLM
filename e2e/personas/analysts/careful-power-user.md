# Analyst: Careful power user

**Who**: Knows the app well and checks everything. From the library's `power-user` and `precision-specialist`.

**Behaviour**: Fills every field, uses search, filters and sorting together, opens details, edits and re-checks them, compares Jev and GPT results field by field, and exports CSVs to check they match the screen.

**Looks for**: Mismatches between list, detail, comparison and export; wrong counts or pagination; numbers (cost, latency, confidence) with inconsistent precision or missing units; and filter state lost on reload.
