**Why it's here**: chart-type-selection reference, organized by the *data relationship* being
shown (Deviation, Correlation, Ranking, Distribution, Change over Time, Magnitude, Part-to-whole,
Spatial, Flow) rather than by chart popularity. Used by `../SKILL.md` §1 (format selection) and
§4 (chart design) to cross-check a project's chart-type mapping.

## Number-formatting conventions (§6 of `../SKILL.md`)

Cross-checked against common financial-reporting practice: right-aligned numbers and consistent
precision down a column; whole-unit currency with K/M/B scaling and one-decimal percentages; and
Lakh/Crore (Indian numbering) as the standard for India-facing INR reporting, versus
Million/Billion for international audiences.
