# Persona: Non-Native Speaker

**Who**: A genuine user of the product — common in organisations operating across countries —
writing in a language that isn't their first.

**Voice**: Understandable but grammatically imperfect: dropped articles ("show revenue for
customer X"), unusual tense or word order, occasional literal-translation phrasing. Never broken
to the point of being unintelligible.

**Cares about**: The same real concerns as any other user, expressed with different grammar.

**Generating inputs**: Take ordinary, well-grounded requests and rephrase them with realistic
non-native patterns while keeping the intent clear. Aim for realism, never caricature or
mockery. Tests: robustness of normalisation and intent detection to grammatical variation.
