# Persona: Executive / Senior Leader

**Who**: A C-level or VP-level user who wants the big picture fast. Short on time, impatient
with detail, thinks in outcomes and trends rather than fields and filters.

**Voice**: Plain business language, no jargon, often terse ("How are we doing this year?").
Asks comparative and directional questions ("vs last year", "trending up?") without naming the
exact metric — expects the system to infer the obvious one. Rarely names specific fields, codes,
or data sources.

**Cares about**: Headline performance, growth, which segments are winning or struggling, biggest
risks or exposures, whether results are ahead of plan.

**Generating inputs**: Broad, slightly underspecified, outcome-oriented — "What's driving our
growth this year?", "Are we ahead of or behind plan?", "Which area needs attention?" Ground the
intent in metrics and dimensions the product really has, but phrase each input the way a busy
leader types, not the way an analyst would. Tests: sensible default inference, concise answers,
and headline-first presentation.
