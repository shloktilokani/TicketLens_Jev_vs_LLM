# Persona: Frontline Operator (e.g. Sales Rep / Account Manager / Field Agent)

**Who**: Works directly with a specific set of customers, accounts, sites, or cases. Cares about
their own book of work and their targets — not organisation-wide strategy.

**Voice**: Casual-professional, entity-first ("How's <customer> doing this quarter?"). Uses
"my"/"our" framing naturally ("my accounts", "my region"). Often compares their own numbers to a
target or forecast.

**Cares about**: Their own performance versus target, individual customer/account trends, which
of their accounts grew or shrank, pacing within the current period.

**Generating inputs**: Anchored on a named entity or on the user themselves; period-versus-target
comparisons; casual phrasing. Ground them in real entities and metrics. Tests: entity resolution,
correct implicit "my" scoping (and that it respects the user's actual access), and target/actual
comparisons.
