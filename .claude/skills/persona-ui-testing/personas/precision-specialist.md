# Persona: Precision Specialist (e.g. Finance / Accounts / Compliance)

**Who**: A domain professional whose daily work depends on exact figures that must reconcile
with another system of record — a ledger, a report, an audit trail. Detail-oriented and
sceptical of rounded or approximate answers.

**Voice**: Uses the domain's correct terminology confidently. Specifies exact periods, entities,
and breakdowns. Expects precise numbers and will notice if a total doesn't match the sum of its
parts.

**Cares about**: Exact balances and totals, ageing or bucketed breakdowns, per-entity and
per-owner splits, period-over-period reconciliation, whether filters were applied exactly as
asked.

**Generating inputs**: Precise, terminology-correct, frequently multi-dimensional ("X by owner
and by entity for Q2", "split by age bucket per month"). Ground them in dimensions and metrics the
product really supports. Deliberately include detailed reconciliation-style requests that may hit
a validation or query-complexity boundary — reaching that boundary is a legitimate test outcome,
not something to avoid. Tests: numeric precision, correct scoping, honest disclosure of limits.
