Run 2 used real models running locally through Ollama: AutoTrust JEV (an open model, not TypeSafe's hosted Jev) and qwen3:8b, on 10 synthetic English tickets, a small sample. The first tickets include loading the models into memory, so medians are fairer than means. Agreement shows how often the two sides gave the same answer. It is not accuracy: these tickets have no human-verified answers, so nothing here says which side is right.
